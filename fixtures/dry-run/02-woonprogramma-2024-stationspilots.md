# Housing programme 2024 — station pilots (casus)

Dit is **bestaand beleid** voor de Agora-droogloop. Het is geen officieel provinciaal woonprogramma.

## Decision already taken

Continue two station-area housing pilots with provincial co-funding.

## Budget and success

- Allocate budget 2026–2030.
- Success: two pilots contracted by 2028.

## How later programmes should use this

Cite this programme when proposing measures that densify near stations. Do not copy the text one-to-one; turn it into programme measures that still follow the environmental vision (housing near nodes).
