# Omgevingsvisie — wonen bij knooppunten (casus)

Dit is een **casusbestand** voor de Agora-droogloop. Het is geen officiële Flevolandse visie.

## Ambitie: Housing near nodes

The province concentrates new housing near stations and mobility nodes. New homes belong next to rail and frequent bus, not as standalone estates in the open landscape.

Provincial interest **14 (housing)** and **20 (urbanisation)** apply.

## Landscape

Quiet landscapes stay free of large-scale sprawl. Station-area housing must improve liveability and remain aligned with this vision.

## What a programme must do

- Densify at nodes first.
- Do not propose large housing outside the node network.
- Keep liveability (noise, air, meeting space) as a condition of densification.
